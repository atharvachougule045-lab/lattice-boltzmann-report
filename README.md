## Overview
This technical report covers the theoretical derivations and computational implementation of the Lattice Boltzmann Method (LBM) for fluid flow simulations. Conducted during a research internship under Prof. M. P. Gururajan in the Department of Metallurgical Engineering and Materials Science at IIT Bombay.

### Key Topics
- Kinetic theory, continuum mechanics, and lattice gas automata
- Lattice Boltzmann Equation (LBE) discretization across D2Q9 and D3Q19 topologies
- Numerical stability criteria ($Ma < 0.3$) and lattice unit conversion
- CPU (OpenMP) vs. GPU (NVIDIA CUDA) benchmarking in OpenLB
